Orbit

A browser-based exploration of movement, form and spatial interaction.

  • Creative direction
  • WebGL
  • 3D

Orbit considers the browser as a place to explore form rather than simply view it. A changing spatial composition invites the visitor to slow down, look around and notice how perspective reshapes a familiar silhouette.

Creative direction, real-time graphics and interaction share a single purpose: making the relationship between movement and space feel immediate. The interface stays quiet so the changing form can lead.

The concept is an open-ended sketch in digital material. Instead of arriving at one final image, it treats each moment of interaction as another possible composition.
