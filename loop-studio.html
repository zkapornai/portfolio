Loop Studio

A visual identity and website shaped by a continuous, expressive motion language.

  • Branding
  • Web development
  • Motion design

Loop Studio explores how a brand can feel in motion as well as on the page. A continuous visual thread gives the identity its character and creates a link between the studio's different touchpoints.

The website brings branding, development and motion into one coherent experience. Transitions guide attention through the content while keeping the underlying structure clear and easy to use.

The concept is built to flex: recognisable as a system, but open enough to keep moving as the studio and its work evolve.
