Tiny Instruments

Small interactive objects that turn simple gestures into playful sound.

  • Physical computing
  • Sound design
  • Prototyping

Tiny Instruments explores the moment when a simple object becomes something to play. Small gestures invite an immediate response, making sound feel connected to the movement that created it.

The prototypes bring physical computing and sound design together in a compact form. Each interaction is kept direct, so the relationship between action and audio stays easy to understand and satisfying to repeat.

Rather than asking for a right way to play, the work leaves space for curiosity. A touch, tilt or tap can become the beginning of a small personal rhythm.
