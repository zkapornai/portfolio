Quiet Hours

A considered space for building calmer routines through sound and gentle prompts.

  • App design
  • Sound design

Quiet Hours is a concept for making room to pause in a day full of interruptions. It approaches routine as an invitation, not a streak to maintain or a task to complete.

Gentle prompts and a considered sound palette work together to make transitions feel softer. The interface aims to be present when needed and unobtrusive when it is not.

By keeping the experience focused on a few small choices, the app creates a quieter starting point for rest, reflection or simply doing one thing at a time.
